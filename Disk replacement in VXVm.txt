		vxdiskadm   - option 4 then y to continue. and the quit finally.
		OR
		vxdisk rm c0t0d0s2
6. unconfigure disk 	
		cfgadm -al 
		cfgadm -c unconfigure c0:/dev/dsk/c0t0d0 (This will blink the LED of hard disk)
7.ask vendor to replace disk and issue ( tail -f /var/adm/messages ) in os side to check if physical drive is getting replaced.

/usr/sbin/drvconfig
/usr/sbin/devlinks
/usr/sbin/disks
/usr/ucb/ucblinks

devfsadm ( sufficient alone in sol 10 )

9.check output ( echo|format ) if output shows new disk

		vxdisk list

# vxdg -k rmdisk disk1 

# vxdg -k adddisk disk1=c1t0d1
